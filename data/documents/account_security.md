# Account Security and Locked Accounts
## Locked accounts
After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. You can unlock it sooner with the "Forgot password" link, which emails a reset link valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

## Staying safe
ShopEase support will never ask for your password or full card number. If you think your account was hacked, our Security team must handle it; support agents cannot unlock accounts or change the account email manually.
