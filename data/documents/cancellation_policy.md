# Order Cancellation Policy
## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for free until its status changes to "shipped". Go to Account > Orders > select the order > Cancel order. Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## After shipping
Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse delivery or return it under the Returns Policy.
