# Shipping and Delivery Policy
## Delivery times
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days.

## Free shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75.

## Tracking
A tracking email is sent when your order is dispatched.

## Late or lost orders
If your order is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, you can ask us to open an investigation. If it is still not delivered 10 business days after the estimate, we will reship it or refund it.
