# Refund Policy
## Refund method and timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after our warehouse receives the returned item.

## Large orders
Orders over $200 need a manual review by our team, which can add up to 3 business days.

## Shipping costs
Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective or the error was ours.

## Store credit
Customers can choose instant store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method.
